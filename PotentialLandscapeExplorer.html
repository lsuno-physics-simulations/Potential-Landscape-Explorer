Probe & physics

Probe is within 0.08 m of an active source. Move it outside the excluded point-charge region.

V = k Σ qᵢ/rᵢ + V₀
E = −∇V   ·   F = qtestE
U = qtestV   ·   Wfield = qtest(Vref − V)

A reference shift changes V and U but leaves ΔV, E, force and work unchanged.

Model assumptions & questions to explore

Two fixed point charges are at (−d/2, 0) and (+d/2, 0). The test charge does not alter the source charges. Regions within 0.08 m of active sources are excluded to avoid singularities; these holes are not conducting spheres. The single-charge preset retains the left source at −d/2.

The surface height and color scale are limited; probe and slice values use the unclipped model. Read the numeric field strength rather than arrow length. With both sources at zero, the potential is the constant V₀ and the field is zero.

  1. For a dipole, can V be zero where E is nonzero?
  2. For two equal positive charges, can E be zero where V is nonzero?
  3. Shift V₀. Which readouts change, and which do not?
  4. Reverse the test charge. What happens to force direction, energy and work?
