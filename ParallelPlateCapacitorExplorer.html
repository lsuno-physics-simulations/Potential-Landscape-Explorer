    Model assumptions & questions to explore

    Ideal parallel plates: no fringing or leakage. A homogeneous, linear dielectric fills the entire gap. All adjustments are quasistatic. The force is the ideal plate attraction; dielectric deformation is excluded.

    Disconnecting preserves the instantaneous free charge on each plate. Reconnecting restores the selected battery voltage. At fixed voltage, both battery energy exchange and mechanical work matter; a change in stored energy alone is not the total battery energy transfer.

    1. Double the gap with the battery connected, then repeat with it disconnected. Compare E and U.
    2. Increase κ in both states. Why does E respond differently?
    3. Increase plate area at fixed charge. What happens to ΔV?
    4. Explain why the field is negative while the potential slope is positive.
